# HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Retention

End-to-end HR analytics project analyzing employee attrition using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

---

## Project Overview

This project examines why employees leave an organization and identifies the key factors that drive attrition.  
The goal is to help HR teams reduce turnover through data-driven insights and targeted retention actions.

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition  
**Employees analyzed:** 1,470  
**Tools:** Python + SQL + Power BI

---

## Key Results

| Metric                        | Value      |
|-------------------------------|------------|
| Total employees               | 1,470      |
| Attrition rate                | 16.12%     |
| Retention rate                | 83.88%     |
| Average tenure                | 7.01 years |
| Highest attrition department  | Sales (20.63%) |
| Overtime attrition rate       | 30.53%     |

---

## High-Impact Insights

- Sales department has the highest attrition rate (20.63%)
- Employees working overtime leave at nearly **3×** the rate of those who do not
- Lowest salary quartile employees have significantly higher attrition
- Low job satisfaction is strongly linked to higher turnover
- Early-career and short-tenure employees are at elevated risk

## Recommended Actions for HR

- Focus retention efforts on the Sales department
- Monitor and manage sustained overtime
- Review compensation competitiveness in lower salary bands
- Conduct stay interviews for employees in their first 3 years
- Track job satisfaction and work-life balance as leading indicators

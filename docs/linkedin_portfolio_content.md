## Post Version

I completed an end-to-end HR Analytics project focused on employee attrition and retention.

Using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset, I analyzed 1,470 employee records to identify the factors most associated with attrition and translate the findings into HR actions.

Key findings:

- Overall attrition rate: 16.12%
- Sales had the highest department attrition rate: 20.63%
- Employees working overtime had 30.53% attrition vs 10.44% without overtime
- Lowest salary quartile attrition was 29.27% vs 10.33% in the highest salary quartile
- Low job satisfaction was linked with higher attrition risk

Tools used:

- Python for data cleaning, feature engineering, segmentation, and analysis
- SQL for HR business questions and KPI validation
- Power BI for dashboard design and executive reporting

Business recommendations included overtime monitoring, Sales-focused stay interviews, compensation review for lower salary bands, and early-tenure retention programs.

Project focus: turning HR data into practical retention decisions.

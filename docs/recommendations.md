# Actionable Recommendations for HR Teams

## 1. Reduce Overtime Exposure

Employees working overtime have a 30.53% attrition rate compared with 10.44% for employees without overtime. HR should build a recurring overtime review with managers and identify teams where overtime is becoming structural rather than occasional.

Recommended actions:

- Track overtime by department and job role every month.
- Flag employees with sustained overtime for manager check-ins.
- Review workload, hiring gaps, and shift coverage in high-overtime groups.

## 2. Prioritize Sales Retention

Sales has the highest department attrition rate at 20.63%. This does not mean every Sales employee is at risk, but it does make Sales the first place to investigate workload, compensation design, management support, and role expectations.

Recommended actions:

- Run Sales-focused stay interviews.
- Compare attrition by Sales job role and salary band.
- Review incentive plans and career path visibility.

## 3. Review Lower Salary Bands

The lowest salary quartile has 29.27% attrition, while the highest salary quartile has 10.33%. Compensation may not be the only driver, but it is a clear risk signal when combined with overtime, low satisfaction, or limited promotion history.

Recommended actions:

- Benchmark compensation for high-risk roles.
- Build retention offers selectively for critical roles.
- Combine pay review with career progression planning.

## 4. Improve Job Satisfaction Follow-Up

Employees with low job satisfaction show 22.84% attrition. Satisfaction scores should be treated as an operational signal, not only an annual survey output.

Recommended actions:

- Create manager action plans for low-satisfaction teams.
- Follow up on satisfaction survey results within 30 days.
- Pair satisfaction trends with attrition, overtime, and promotion history.

## 5. Support Early-Tenure Employees

Attrition risk is commonly higher among newer employees and employees with limited company tenure. Early intervention can prevent avoidable exits before employees disengage.

Recommended actions:

- Add structured 30/60/90-day onboarding checks.
- Run stay interviews for employees in the first three years.
- Monitor role clarity, manager support, and learning opportunities.
